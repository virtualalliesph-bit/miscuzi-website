Our Story

A Family Recipe, Made to be Shared

Miscuzi began in 2017, when our founder, Nicel, returned to Manila after years of flying in the Middle East.

It started with a mother's cravings, a sister's recipe, and a family's love for lasagna.

What began in their home kitchen has grown into a signature dish made for sharing and loved by many.
